About Me

Introduction

My name is John Bernie Laborte, a visually impaired individual who aims to share knowledge, tips, and practical information about using computers, mobile phones, software, screen readers, and other technologies that may be useful to fellow visually impaired users.

Through this website, I want to share what I have learned in a simple, clear, and understandable way. My goal is not to make technology seem complicated, but to help readers understand and use the tools and technologies available to them.

The Drive

It has been several years since I first learned how to use computers, mobile phones, and software commonly used by the general public. Looking back, I cannot help but compare the experience I had when I was learning with what is available today.

Back then, if you wanted to learn how to use a computer or a mobile phone, you often had to find someone who already knew how to use it, wait for someone to teach you, or hope for an opportunity to attend a training program.

If you were able to join a training, you were fortunate. If you were not, you had to find another way. And even when you found someone willing to teach you, the experience could still depend on how well that person could explain things in a way that was understandable and accessible to you.

Today, we have resources that were not as readily available before, including AI tools that can provide explanations and guidance when you need them. Learning independently has become easier in many ways. But even before these tools became widely available, there were already video tutorials and other learning materials online.

The problem was that these materials were not always created with visually impaired users in mind. Some tutorials assumed that the learner could see what was happening on the screen, while others did not provide enough detail for someone trying to follow the process non-visually. In many cases, learning still required a great deal of personal trial and error, finding someone who could teach you, or waiting for an opportunity to receive proper training.

The Mission

Through this personal blog, I want to share what I know and continue learning alongside the people who visit this site.

I was given opportunities to learn many of the things I know today, and I believe that what I have learned should also be shared with others who may not have had the same opportunities.

Here, I will share practical knowledge about technology, tips and tricks that I have learned, experiences from using technology as a visually impaired person, and things I have discovered along the way.

Some of the topics I write about may come from things I have personally learned through experience, while others may come from problems I have encountered and eventually figured out how to solve.

I do not claim to know everything, and I cannot teach everything. But if there is something I know that can help another visually impaired person learn something they previously did not know how to do, then I believe it is worth sharing.
